# Interstice Systems

**Interstice Systems** is an experimental engineering lab exploring the intersection of software, systems, automation, and simulation.

The goal is simple: build interesting systems, explore technical ideas, and develop tools that operate at the boundaries between disciplines.

---

## Philosophy

Modern technology rarely exists in isolation.
The most interesting problems emerge where systems overlap:

* software and machines
* automation and infrastructure
* simulation and real-world processes

Interstice Systems exists to explore and build within these spaces.

---

## Areas of Exploration

Interstice projects may explore topics such as:

* systems engineering
* operational tooling and automation
* monitoring and diagnostics
* simulation and modeling
* interactive software and game development

---

## Status

Interstice Systems is currently in **early development** as an independent experimental workspace.

Projects and experiments will be added as they are developed.
